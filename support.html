Support

A useful report starts with context.

For product support, email dragon_kang_app@163.com.

Product support by email.

This page has no contact form or account registration. A mailto link opens your email app; nothing is sent automatically. Do not email private keys, license files or confidential inspected files.

Bug report

Prepare the app version, macOS version, Mac architecture, short steps to reproduce, expected behavior and observed behavior. Include the app’s sanitized Copy Error Details when available.

Feature request

Describe the developer task you want to complete, the file format involved and what an improved inspection would help you understand.

Privacy question

Read the Privacy page for current processing, recent history and clipboard behavior. Send any remaining product privacy question to the support email above.

Sanitize before sharing

Use synthetic examples where possible. Remove personal certificate identities, device identifiers, team identifiers, private paths, secrets and private IPA contents from any report or screenshot. Do not share private keys, passwords or signing credentials.

Start with the known limits

Certificate dates do not establish trust. IPA metadata is not signature or installation validation. Profile parsing does not assert Apple trust. See the FAQ and pre-release candidate notes.
